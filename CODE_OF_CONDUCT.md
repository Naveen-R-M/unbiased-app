# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Circuit & Chisel is committed to an open, welcoming, and professional community.
Contributors must communicate respectfully, give constructive feedback, and
avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or disclosure of another person's
private information.

Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- Focusing feedback on the work rather than the person.
- Respecting different viewpoints, experiences, and levels of expertise.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing when appropriate, and learning from
  mistakes.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, or unwanted sexual
  attention.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of project discussions.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual is
officially representing a Circuit & Chisel project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a Circuit & Chisel organization owner
through GitHub. Do not disclose sensitive details in a public issue.

Project maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect the privacy and
safety of reporters where possible, and may edit or remove contributions or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions are
made by maintainers and organization owners.
